# Research Overview

My research focuses on applying health economics, statistical modelling, and
real-world data to understand how diseases and treatments affect healthcare
utilization, clinical outcomes, employment, productivity, and economic outcomes.

My work spans:

- Health economics and economic evaluation
- Real-world evidence (RWE)
- Health technology assessment (HTA)
- Healthcare resource utilization (HCRU)
- Treatment patterns and persistence
- Rare disease research
- Administrative health databases
- Longitudinal and panel data
- Survival and time-to-event analysis
- Difference-in-differences
- Mixed and hierarchical models
- Cost and economic models
- Health outcomes research
- Labor-market and productivity outcomes
